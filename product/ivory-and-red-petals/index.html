Designing the memories that matter

Crafting Dreams began with a simple belief: life's biggest moments, like a wedding garland, a newborn's tiny hands or a festival with family, deserve to be kept beautifully.

We turn them into resin art you can hold, hang and gift. No two pieces are exactly alike, and that's what makes them yours.

Questions, answered

How long does an order take?

Most pieces take 5–10 working days including curing time. Varamala and baby casting pieces can take longer; we'll confirm the timeline on WhatsApp.

How do I customise my order?

Choose your item and pay online. After payment, message us on WhatsApp with your names, dates and colours, and we'll confirm the design before we start.

How do I pay?

Pay securely at checkout with UPI, cards or netbanking through Razorpay.

How much is delivery?

Delivery charges depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp.

How do I care for my resin piece?

Keep it away from direct sunlight and heat, and wipe it gently with a soft, dry cloth.
